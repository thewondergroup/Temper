The room

The original since 1991

This is where Big Easy started: 332 King's Road, 1991, exposed brick, filament bulbs, benches you share with strangers and a bar you don't leave. Chelsea's been eating lobster with its hands here for thirty-odd years.

We kept the brick, the benches, the bar and the band. We brought the temper fire and the temper meat.
