What’s the minimum group size for bookings?
6+ is typically considered a group booking. For private rooms or set menus, enquire directly — we’re flexible.

Can we choose from the main menu?
Yes, though group menus are available and make things easier (and better value) for larger tables.

Do you cater for dietary needs?
Yes — vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, no spice, all handled. Just let us know when booking.

Is temper good for corporate groups or team dinners?
Absolutely. From post-work feasts to department blowouts, we handle it all — food, drinks, service.

Can we hire out the restaurant?
Yes. Full and partial venue hire available in all three sites. Contact us for bespoke options.
