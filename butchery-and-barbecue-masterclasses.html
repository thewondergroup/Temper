Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Do I need experience?
No. Complete beginners to keen home cooks.
Is it hands-on?
No. You watch professional butchers, ask questions and take it home. Smart casual, nothing to bring.
What meat?
Mainly beef: cuts, breeds, dry-ageing. Pork and lamb come up too.
Vegetarian?
It's a meat-led evening. Flag dietary needs when booking.
How do I book?
DesignMyNight above, or contact City. A discretionary 15% service charge applies.
