What you get

Demo. Feast. Done.

Train alongside accredited butchers in a working London restaurant. Cattle breeds, provenance, the nose-to-tail approach; watch them work, ask anything, then eat the results.

  • Beef cuts and how each one wants cooking
  • Judging quality by marbling, colour and texture
  • What dry-ageing does, and why we do it in-house
  • Basic knife skills and how breed changes taste

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Do I need experience?
No. It runs from complete beginners to keen home cooks.
Is it hands-on?
No. You watch professional butchers, ask questions and take it home. Smart casual, nothing to bring.
What meat is covered?
Mainly beef: cuts, breeds, dry-ageing. Pork and lamb come up too.
Vegetarian options?
It's a meat-led evening. Flag dietary needs when booking and we'll talk.
How do I book?
Through DesignMyNight above, or contact City directly. A discretionary 15% service charge applies.
