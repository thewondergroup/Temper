Rare-breed beef & in-house butchery

Our meat. One farm, whole animals, no shortcuts.

Everything we cook comes from Charles Ashbridge's farm in North Yorkshire, as it has since 2016. We buy the whole animal, butcher it ourselves and dry-age every cut.

Sustainability

We don't pay to offset and call it green.

We work with farmers who manage land regeneratively: planting trees, leaving meadows, keeping industrial fertiliser and heavy machinery to a minimum. We only buy from English farms raising native and rare breeds, animals that live longer, happier lives, and we keep the food miles short.

Ageing

Sixty days, on average.

Since December 2022 every steak has been aged in-house, which means total control and a consistent result. The minimum is 28 days, the average is 60, and the chefs will push a cut further when it earns it.

Fancy a birthday steak? Talk to us, choose your cuts, set the ageing period, and we'll have it ready for the day.

Butchery

When they're gone, they're gone.

We never buy pre-cut steaks. Each animal yields a fixed number of cuts, so when the day's steaks sell out, that's it until the next beast.
