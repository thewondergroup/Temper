Rare-breed beef & in-house butchery

Our meat.

One farm, whole animals, no shortcuts. Everything we cook comes from Charles Ashbridge in North Yorkshire, as it has since 2016. We buy the whole beast, butcher it ourselves and dry-age every cut.

Butchery

When they're gone, they're gone.

We never buy pre-cut steaks. Each animal yields a fixed number of cuts, so when the day's steaks sell out, that's it until the next beast. Average yield per animal:

Rib-eye7kg · 14 × 500g
Rump4.5kg · 15 × 300g
Porterhouse4kg · 8 × 500g
T-bone4kg · 8 × 500g
Picanha1.2kg · 4 × 300g
Château900g · 3 × 300g

Sixty days, on average.

Aged in-house since December 2022 · 28 days minimum · chefs push a cut further when it earns it

Sustainability

We don't pay to offset and call it green.

We work with farmers who manage land regeneratively: planting trees, leaving meadows, keeping industrial fertiliser and heavy machinery to a minimum. We only buy from English farms raising native and rare breeds, animals that live longer, happier lives, and we keep the food miles short.

Fancy a birthday steak? Talk to us, choose your cuts, set the ageing period, and it'll be ready on the day.
