Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Where is temper Soho?
25 Broadwick Street, W1F 0DF, by Carnaby Street. Three minutes from Oxford Circus, six from Piccadilly Circus.
Do I need to book?
For weekend dinners and Sunday lunch, yes. Walk-ins are welcome when there's space, and Taco Tuesday from 5pm is built for walking in.
What's different about the steak?
All of it is cooked over open fire in custom pits. No gas. The beef comes from one farmer in North Yorkshire and is dry-aged and butchered in-house.
Opening hours?
Tuesday to Saturday 12 to 10.15pm, Sunday 12 to 6.15pm for the roast, Monday 12 to 9pm.
Where should I sit?
Counter seats face the fire. Booths suit groups who want a bit of privacy.
Is the Covent Garden temper still open?
No. That one closed; Soho is twelve minutes' walk. temper is opening Bar-B-Q & Crabshack on Maiden Lane in Covent Garden.
