Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Where is temper Soho?
25 Broadwick Street, W1F 0DF, right by Carnaby Street. Three minutes from Oxford Circus, six from Piccadilly Circus.
Do I need to book?
Strongly recommended for weekend dinners and Sunday lunch. Walk-ins welcome when there's space, and Taco Tuesday from 5pm is walk-in friendly.
What's different about the steak?
All of it is cooked over open fire in custom pits, with no gas. The beef comes from one farmer in North Yorkshire and is dry-aged and butchered in-house.
What are the opening hours?
Tuesday to Saturday 12 to 10.15pm, Sunday 12 to 6.15pm for the roast, Monday 12 to 9pm.
Where should I sit?
Counter seats face the fire pits. Booths suit groups who want a bit of privacy.
Is the Covent Garden temper still open?
No. The old Covent Garden temper has closed; Soho is a twelve-minute walk away. temper is now opening Bar-B-Q & Crabshack in Covent Garden on Maiden Lane.
